# Content Review Notes v2

Total items: 535

## Status

This is a large starter expansion for the normal French side of Frenchie. It is good for private testing and app development. It should receive a final human pass before being treated as polished public curriculum.

## Strong coverage

- A2 reinforcement: present tense, prepositions, simple questions, correction basics
- A2+: passé composé, imparfait, passé composé vs imparfait, negation, simple object pronouns
- B1: polite conditional, comparisons, relative pronouns, simple reading, short writing
- B1+: subjunctive present, y/en, indirect speech, hypotheses with si
- B2: connectors, register, formal phrasing, argumentation/writing prompts
- Dictées: visual dictation prompts across A2 to B2

## Known limitations

- Many items are template-based by design so the app can test mechanics and search at scale.
- Audio dictée is not included, only visual dictée text.
- Free writing still needs either self-correction checklist or later AI support.
- B2 material is starter-level only, not complete B2 mastery.
- Some accepted-answer logic will be needed for correction/writing/dictée activities.
